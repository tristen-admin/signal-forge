HOUSE STYLE

Painterly digital fantasy character illustration, semi-realistic rendering, dramatic moody lighting, dark atmospheric background (torchlight, mist, or fog). Portrait orientation, ~4:5 aspect ratio (1024x1280). Desaturated overall palette with one or two saturated accent colors -- cool blue/teal for spectral or magical subjects, warm orange/red for fire, blood, or aggression, muted browns/steel/greens for plain martial subjects. Single character, 3/4 or full-body dynamic pose, background that clearly depicts a real place, subtle dark vignette toward the edges. Registers like Magic: The Gathering or Gwent card art -- not photographic, not flat vector, not anime-cel.
