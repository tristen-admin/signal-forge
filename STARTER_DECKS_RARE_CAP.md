# Starter Decks — Rare-and-Below Redesign

*Compiled 2026-07-28. Three 20-card decklists for the Ironsworn/Broodswarm/Black Wings starter paths, rebuilt from the full Common/Uncommon/Rare pool (118 eligible cards checked) with zero Ultra or Apex cards — a direct reversal of the 7/15/26 "each starter carries exactly 1 Ultra" decision (Sirefax the Warden, Corvus, and Mordezh, Blood Prophet are all removed). Not deployed to the game yet — this is a proposal for you to mark up.*

**Method:** started from each archetype's own real `ARCHETYPE_PRESETS` roster (the cards that actually carry that archetype's identity), filled every deck to the 20-card max with hand-picked glue cards chosen for either direct thematic synergy or clean standalone power, and checked the resulting cost curve and average base power against the design doc's own "cost ≤3 should be ≥50% of the deck" rule. All three land in the same power band (avg base power 9.15–10.0, avg cost 1.45–1.6) — none is a strictly weaker "beginner trap" than the others.

## The Ironsworn — *Strength in Numbers*
Deck Master: **Bannerlord Cassian**

Cheap bodies, go wide, reward every ally on the field and in the Winners Circle. The most forgiving deck to pilot — almost nothing here requires a specific game state to be good, it just wants a full board.

**Suggested swaps to weigh in on:**
- *Josef* is the least essential include — a straight power/cost payoff with no Muster tie-in. **Alternates:** Yard Sparring Partner (cheaper Called+3 glue) if you want the curve even lower, or Squad 19 Vanguard (Called +3, Tosa Pact flavor) if you'd rather lean into the Cadet Corps naming.
- *Courtroom Colossus* is the deck's only 3-cost outside Cassian/Reyva Vosh — if you'd rather keep this deck strictly 1–2 cost for a true "beginner can't mess up the curve" feel, cut it for a second charge piece or Ilma the Patient (also a late-scaling Rare, cheaper at 2).

## The Black Wings — *Banish & Reclaim*
Deck Master: **Kiana**

High-risk, high-reward: feed your own banish zone, then either cash it in directly (power-per-banished-card payoffs) or pull cards back out with a bonus attached. Ossian Drell (the archetype's real boss/rep card) and Corvus (the removed Ultra) both stay out — Kiana anchors it instead, same as today.

**Curve:** 14×cost-1, 5×cost-2, 2×cost-3, 0×cost-4. 100% at cost ≤3, 70% at cost 1 — the cheapest, most aggressive of the three, matching "sacrifice early, reap later."

**Suggested swaps to weigh in on:**
- This deck is already stuffed with banish-fill (Rhaess Korvain, Rook, Grave-Tithe Acolyte, The Black Ledger) and banish-payoff (Falk, Cinder-Marked Hound, Squad-Captain Vesk, Wingblade Scout, Bixie Bee) — arguably *too* many small payoff bodies and not enough reclaim. **Consider cutting** Wingblade Scout or Grave-Warden for a 2nd Ashen-Reclaimer-style effect if one exists you'd rather print, or for Old Garrick (kills/deaths scaling — no banish synergy, but solid raw stats as a stabilizer).
- Gravecaller Voss's Death-Remnant angle is a genuinely different sub-plan (attrition, not banish-count) grafted onto this deck — flagging in case you'd rather keep the deck's identity singular and swap her for a straight banish-payoff instead (e.g., a 4th copy-equivalent of the Falk/Cinder-Marked Hound effect isn't available, so the next best fit is Marrow Priest, though it's Remnant-flavored too).

## The Broodswarm — *Swarm & Overwhelm*
Deck Master: **Broodmother**

Draw, hoard, and convert a full hand directly into power. La "La" Ballora (the archetype's real boss/rep) and Mordezh, Blood Prophet (the removed Ultra) both stay out — Broodmother anchors it instead, same as today. This is the only one of the three with **zero cost-3+ cards at all**, which is a deliberate read of the archetype's own pitch (never spend big, always hold) rather than an oversight — flagged below in case you disagree.

|---|---|---|---|
| Broodmother | 2 | 13 | +1 per hand card; Called Fighter +3 then draw 1 — the anchor |
| Venombrood Queen | 2 | 10 | +2 per hand card |
| The Gathering Swarm | 2 | 14 | +4 at 3+ hand cards, opponent −4 at 5+ — the deck's biggest single payoff |
| Envoy of Tyrants, Ella Ballora | 2 | 14 | +3 at 3+ hand cards, +3 more if you've lost a duel |
| Hoarder's Gambit | 2 | 12 | Draws 2, then +1 per hand card — refuels the exact resource this deck wants |
| Web-Tender Matron | 2 | 9 | Called: draw 2 — pure refuel |
| Web-Weaver's Return | 2 | 11 | Shuffles banish back into deck + draws — keeps the deck from running dry |
| Signal Diviner | 2 | 12 | Charge engine (shared with Ironsworn) |
| Faye Quicksilver | 2 | 13 | Snowballs on wins (shared with Ironsworn) |
| Ilma the Patient | 2 | 13 | +5 from the 3rd commit on — rewards exactly the patient, hoarding plan |
| Fledgling Broodmother | 1 | 6 | +1 per hand card |
| Web-Spinner Acolyte | 1 | 8 | +1 per hand card; Called draw 1 |
| Skittering Drone | 1 | 5 | +1 per hand card |
| Hatchling Tide | 1 | 7 | +4 flat at 4+ hand cards |
| Silkbound Sentinel | 1 | 7 | Called: draw + Fighter +2 |
| Widow's Patience | 1 | 9 | +5 flat at a full (6-card) hand |
| Veronica | 1 | 8 | Copies the opponent's ability for the duel — flexible tech |
| Silk-Fed Hatchling | 1 | 6 | Flat Charge gain |
| Broodweb Keeper | 1 | 5 | Called: Charge if hand is 4+ |
| Melanie | 1 | 1 | Called Fighter +5 & draw (shared with Ironsworn) |

**Curve:** 10×cost-1, 10×cost-2, 0×cost-3+. 100% at cost ≤2 — by far the flattest, lowest curve of the three. That's a direct read of "never spend big, always hold cards" being the actual game plan, not a gap — but it does mean this deck has no single bomb to close a stalled game the way Courtroom Colossus or Valcarion do for the other two.

**Suggested swaps to weigh in on — this is the one I'd most want your read on:**
- **Should this deck get a real top-end at all?** If yes, Bram the Bulwark (Rare, 3-cost, Last Stand — a loss by ≤3 becomes a tie) is a clean, archetype-neutral finisher that doesn't fight the hand-size plan (it's good regardless of hand size). If no, I'd leave it exactly as-is — the flat curve is arguably the whole point.
- *Veronica* and *Silk-Fed Hatchling* are the two least hand-size-synergistic includes (a flexible tech card and a flat Charge body respectively). If you'd rather every card in this deck touch hand-size directly, cut them for a 2nd copy-equivalent of Web-Spinner Acolyte's effect (none exists distinctly, so the honest alternative is doubling down on Broodweb Keeper-style Charge, which doesn't solve the "every card synergizes" ask either) — flagging that this deck may have already tapped out its purely-on-theme pool at 18 real cards, and the last 2 slots are necessarily glue.

## Cross-deck comparison

| Path | Avg base pow | Avg cost | Cost-1 share | Cost-3+ share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ironsworn | 9.5 | 1.6 | 55% | 15% |
| Black Wings | 10.0 | 1.45 | 70% | 10% |
| Broodswarm | 9.15 | 1.5 | 50% | 0% |

All three are within one power point of each other on raw base stats — the real power swings in this game come from each card's conditional bonus firing (Muster count, hand size, banish count, Winners Circle count), not base power alone, so this table is a sanity check that none is starting from a strictly weaker foundation, not the whole balance picture.

**Open question across all three:** Kiana ("Wings in the Dark": draw 1, next conjure +5) and Broodmother ("The Brood Answers": draw 1, next conjure +5) both carry real, identical-shaped DM Activated Abilities, so Black Wings and Broodswarm each already teach that half of the DM system. Bannerlord Cassian has none — Ironsworn's starter is passive-only. If you want all three starters to teach the Activated-Ability button, Ironsworn is the one that needs either a real ability written for Cassian or a Deck Master swap to a member who already has one (none of the other 14 Ironsworn members carry one either, per a quick check — this would be new content, not a swap).
